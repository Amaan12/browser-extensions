Quick launcher to open all extensions in Chrome Web Store tabs.

⚠️ Pop-ups were blocked by your browser

Browsers (especially Brave and Chrome) block websites from opening 16 tabs at once unless you give permission:

  1. Look at the right side of your address bar (or click the Shield icon in Brave).
  2. Click the icon with a red cross: "Pop-ups blocked".
  3. Select "Always allow pop-ups and redirects from this site" and click Done.
  4. Click the green button again — all 16 tabs will open!
